# Answers and grading rubrics

**Evaluator only. Candidates must not read this document during a run.**

Rubric revision: `1`. Case IDs match QUESTIONS.md. Apply the README fair-play review before scoring: confirmed cheating invalidates the entire run. Otherwise, award `1` for an accepted answer with all required explanation criteria, `0.5` for an accepted answer without a sufficient explanation, and `0` for an incorrect or missing answer. Maximum score: `3`.

The source-language answers and explanations below preserve the wordplay. Accept equivalent phrasing, not unrelated alternative jokes. Judge the submitted explanation; use available thinking and tool records to review fair play. Do not require a particular wording or an exact television adaptation/date to explain a joke correctly.

## SB-0001

**Reference answer:** 因为刚擒住了几个妖。

**Explanation:** “钢琴”与“刚擒”谐音；“刚擒住了几个妖”借用了《西游记》相关歌曲中的歌词，“妖”又呼应恐怖片中的妖怪意象。

**Answer acceptance:** The answer must invoke “刚擒住了几个妖” or a close paraphrase retaining “刚擒” and the captured monsters. Describing how piano music creates suspense does not match the intended punchline.

**Required explanation criteria:**

- Identify the sound correspondence between “钢琴” and “刚擒”.
- Recognize the Journey to the West lyric reference and connect the monsters to the horror-film setup. Naming the exact song, adaptation, opening sequence, or broadcast year is not required.

**Scoring boundaries:** The reference punchline alone, or with only a generic claim that it is a pun, earns `0.5`. The accepted punchline plus the specific sound correspondence and contextual reference earns `1`. An answer about eerie piano music without the intended punchline earns `0`.

## SB-0002

**Reference answer:** 工藤新一，因为他不会忘记小兰。

**Explanation:** “不会忘记小兰”谐音“不会忘纪晓岚”。工藤新一与毛利兰的关系提供“不会忘记小兰”的语境，再通过“记小兰／纪晓岚”的谐音，转成对纪晓岚的怀念。

**Answer acceptance:** Accept 工藤新一, 新一, or 柯南／江户川柯南 when used as the same character. The character alone is an accepted answer but needs a qualifying explanation for full credit. A different character or a historical acquaintance of Ji Xiaolan does not match the intended answer.

**Required explanation criteria:**

- Connect Shinichi (Conan) to Ran and the idea that he would not forget her.
- Explicitly connect “忘记小兰” with “忘纪晓岚”, or equivalently identify “记小兰／纪晓岚” as the sound-based bridge from that relationship to the question.

**Scoring boundaries:** An accepted character with only “he misses Ran”, only a generic mention of a homophone, or no explanation earns `0.5`. The accepted character with both the relationship and the specific homophone earns `1`. An incorrect character earns `0` even if the explanation mentions the pun.

## SB-0003

**Reference answer:** “现在该你拿了，谁考第一谁拿着。”

**Explanation:** 题目让人以为原来的年级第一因被超越而来报复，棍子像是打人的工具；答案却把它解释成由年级第一轮流拿着的东西，他只是来交接。笑点是从威胁、报复的预期反转为“谁第一谁拿棍子”的规则，结构类似经典的摸鼻子梗。

**Answer acceptance:** Accept dialogue conveying that the stick is being handed over because the top-ranked student must hold it, for example “你现在第一了，这根棍子归你拿着”. A threat, congratulation alone, or handing over a stick without a connection to first place does not satisfy the answer rule.

**Required explanation criteria:**

- Identify the setup's apparent threat or revenge by the displaced top-ranked student.
- Explain the reversal: the stick is passed to whoever ranks first, so the visit is a routine handover rather than an attack. Recognize this as the joke's invented rule, not an established real-world school custom.

**Scoring boundaries:** An accepted handover line without an explanation of the reversal earns `0.5`. The accepted line plus both the threatening expectation and the handover reinterpretation earns `1`. A retaliation punchline earns `0`. Naming or knowing the nose-touching joke is not required.
